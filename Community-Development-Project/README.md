# Community Development Project

## Overview

This folder contains the complete framework, project deliverables, and supporting documentation for the **ICDFA Community Development Project**. The initiative was designed to strengthen the institution's cybersecurity posture, enhance risk awareness, and foster a culture of governance, risk management, and compliance (GRC).

The project combined policy development, cybersecurity awareness, technical risk assessment, implementation planning, and stakeholder collaboration to deliver a practical roadmap for improving organizational security and resilience.

---

# My Role – Presentation & Reporting Team

As a member of the **Presentation & Reporting Team**, I played a central role in coordinating and consolidating project deliverables from the various functional teams involved in the Community Development Project.

My responsibilities included:

- Coordinating with all project teams to gather completed deliverables and supporting documentation.
- Reviewing reports for consistency, completeness, and professional presentation.

This role required strong organizational, communication, documentation, and collaboration skills, as well as the ability to work effectively with multiple teams to produce a unified and high-quality project deliverable.

---

# Project Objectives

The Community Development Project aimed to:

- Strengthen cybersecurity governance within the institution.
- Improve awareness of cybersecurity threats and secure practices.
- Identify, assess, and prioritize organizational cybersecurity risks.
- Recommend appropriate technical and administrative security controls.
- Develop practical implementation and risk treatment plans.
- Promote compliance with recognized cybersecurity best practices and industry standards.
