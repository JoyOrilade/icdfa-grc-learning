# Phase 2 – Advanced Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

## Overview

Phase 2 of the International Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA) builds upon the foundational knowledge gained in Phase 1 by introducing more advanced Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) concepts, practical cybersecurity assessments, and collaborative project work.

This phase emphasizes the practical application of cybersecurity governance, risk management, compliance frameworks, and organizational security practices through hands-on laboratories, technical documentation, and community-based cybersecurity initiatives.

---

## Learning Objectives

During this phase, I aim to:

- Apply advanced Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) principles.
- Conduct cybersecurity risk assessments and analyses.
- Develop organizational security documentation and policies.
- Strengthen cybersecurity awareness through community engagement.
- Improve technical documentation and reporting skills.
- Collaborate effectively in multidisciplinary cybersecurity teams.
- Apply industry frameworks and best practices to real-world scenarios.
