
This folder contains assignments, practical exercises, and learning materials for the GRC 105 Incident Management and Business Continuity module completed during Phase 2 of the ICDFA programme.

| `GRC 105_IRP ASSIGNMENT_JOY ORILADE` | Incident Response Planning assignment (Tasks 1 to 6) based on a fictional suspected ransomware incident at Northbridge Skills Institute |

## Assignment overview

The Incident Response Planning assignment covers:

- **Task 1:** Incident classification and initial assessment, including an evidence-linked timeline, severity rating, CIA impact assessment and priority investigation questions
- **Task 2:** Incident response policy and plan outline, CSIRT roles, RACI matrix and a readiness checklist
- **Task 3:** Response playbook for the first 60 minutes and first 24 hours, evidence register and chain of custody, eradication and recovery
- **Task 4:** Communication matrix, executive briefing, notification obligations and escalation rules
- **Task 5:** Tabletop exercise design, walk-through and improvement plan
- **Task 6:** Case study analysis and critical reflection

## Frameworks and references

- NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- CISA ransomware guidance and tabletop exercise packages
- Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023

> All organisations, people and exercise results in this assignment are fictional
